# Spurvance Labs – Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Spurvance Labs is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and
professional environment for everyone participating in our ecosystem.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, employees, contractors, and community
members to uphold these standards in all interactions related to Spurvance Labs.

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in corrective action, including
removal from projects or revocation of access.

---

## 2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Spurvance Labs spaces, including:

- Source code repositories, issues, pull requests, and reviews
- Community discussions (chat platforms, forums, social media)
- Official events, meetings, or workshops
- Public representation of Spurvance Labs

---

## 3. Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and patient
- Welcome people of all backgrounds and identities
- Engage in constructive technical discussion
- Accept feedback gracefully and give feedback professionally
- Practice intellectual honesty and humility
- Focus on collaboration, learning, and shared goals

---

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind
- Offensive comments related to gender, identity, race, religion, disability,
  age, or sexual orientation
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or encouragement of self-harm
- Deliberate misgendering or refusal to respect stated names or pronouns
- Sustained disruption of discussions or project work
- Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate content
- Publishing private information without consent
- Plagiarism, falsification of data, or misrepresentation of work

---

## 5. Reporting & Enforcement

### Reporting
If you experience or witness a violation, please report it confidentially:

📧 **conduct@spurvancelabs.com**

You may include:
- Names or usernames involved
- Description of the incident
- Relevant links, screenshots, or timestamps

Anonymous reports are accepted, though they may limit investigation.

---

### Enforcement Process

Reports are reviewed by designated Spurvance Labs maintainers or leadership.

Actions may include:
- Warning or guidance
- Temporary or permanent removal from the project
- Revocation of access or roles

All reports are handled with confidentiality and fairness.

---

## 6. Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly
prohibited and will result in immediate corrective action.

---
