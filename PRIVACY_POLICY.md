## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide
We collect data you voluntarily provide, such as:

- **Account Data:** Name, username, email, password  
- **Developer Metadata:** GitHub/GitLab handles, public SSH keys, job titles  
- **Public Contributions:** Git commits, pull requests, comments  
- **Payment Data:** Billing addresses and payment information (via third-party processors)  

### Information Automatically Collected
We also collect technical and usage data automatically:

- IP address, browser, device type, OS, language preferences  
- Referring URLs, usage activity, error reports  
- Device event data and system configurations  

### Information from Other Sources
We may obtain information from:

- Public databases, partners, social media platforms  
- Affiliate programs and third-party services  

**Note:** Data collected is only used for providing services, community engagement, and improving our products.

---

## 2. How We Process Your Information

We use personal information for:

- Delivering Services and Programs  
- Managing Open Source Contributions and CLAs  
- Sending updates, newsletters, and impact reports  
- Coordinating volunteers and partners  
- Improving program effectiveness and product performance  
- Ensuring safety, security, and legal compliance  

**Telemetry:**  
- Only anonymized data is collected  
- Includes command success/failure, OS, CPU, runtime version, feature usage  
- Users can opt-out via environment variable, config file, or CLI flag

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

- **Third-party service providers**: cloud services, analytics, payment processors  
- **Law enforcement or regulatory authorities** in Pakistan under valid legal requests  
- **Affiliates and program partners** for legitimate business purposes  
- **Other users**: when contributing to public repositories or interacting in public areas  

**Note:** Your Git commit history and profile info are permanently public.

## 9. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Purpose |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Git Commit History | Indefinite | Open-source licensing and authorship verification |
| SaaS Account Data | Until deletion | Platform access |
